# Code of Conduct

We want AIRFlow to be a welcoming, respectful place for everyone who takes part,
whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, also when you disagree.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit the ideas and work of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@jvanlangen](https://github.com/jvanlangen)
through GitHub. Reports are handled with care and kept confidential where possible.

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and
discussions.
